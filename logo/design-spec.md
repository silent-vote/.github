# Logo Design Specification — PEOPLE / Barcode

## 1. Design Overview

**Concept:**  
A minimalist barcode-based logo combining **commerce, human participation, scanning, and conscious choice**.

**Core visual metaphor:**

> **People → Choice → Commerce → System**

The logo consists of three integrated elements:

1. **Barcode** — represents products, commerce, corporate systems, and supply chains.
2. **Red scanning laser** — represents the moment of transaction and interaction with the system.
3. **Hand + checkmarked ballot** — represents a person making a conscious choice.
4. **Hidden Morse encoding** — the barcode itself subtly encodes `PEOPLE`.
